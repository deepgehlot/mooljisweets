OUR STORY

A Family Legacy

Guided By Generations Of Trust And Taste

The journey of Moolji Sweets began in the 1890s when Seth Shri Shankarlalji Dhoot started the business in Juni Mandi, inside Jodhpur's walled city. In 1911, the store moved to Sarafa Bazar and was officially established as Moolji ki Hotel, a name that became part of Old City's food memories.

After his passing in 1966, his sons took charge at a very young age. With honest work, dedication, and the love of the people, they protected the original taste and took their father's and grandfather's legacy to new heights.

Today, the legacy continues with the next generation of the Dhoot family. Combining traditional recipes with modern ideas, Moolji Sweets continues to preserve the authentic taste, purity, and trust of Jodhpur.

"We do not just make sweets. We carry forward the trust of Jodhpur, one batch at a time."

The Dhoot Family Custodians of Moolji Sweets' Legacy
